RESELL / HELP

How to use this.

Short, step-by-step instructions for everything you'll do. You can't break anything — every step can be fixed or undone later.

When you buy something to resell

  1. Tap Home in the menu, then the big I bought something button.
  2. Type a short name, like “Coach purse, brown leather”.
  3. Enter the date and what you paid.
  4. Pick where you bought it. If it's somewhere new, type the name in the box just below the list.
  5. Tap Photos to take pictures or choose them from your phone. You can add more later.
  6. Tap Save this item at the bottom. Done!

Bought a bag of 10 things at once? Add each item separately and split what you paid between them as best you can.

When you list it for sale

  1. On Home, tap I listed something.
  2. Find the item and tap I listed it.
  3. Pick the site (eBay, Poshmark…) and type your asking price.
  4. Optional: paste the link to your listing so you can jump to it later.
  5. Tap Save the listing.

Listed on more than one site? Repeat these steps once for each site. That's called cross-listing, and it helps things sell faster.

When something sells

  1. On Home, tap Something sold!
  2. Find the item and tap It sold.
  3. Open the sale on the selling site's app. Copy the sale price, shipping and fees into the boxes.
  4. Tap Record the sale. You'll see your profit straight away.
  5. Important: if it was listed on other sites too, take it down there. The home page will remind you under “Needs your attention”. Tap Done, I took it down once you have.

Don't know the fees? Leave that box empty and they'll be estimated. Typing the real number makes your profit more accurate.

When a buyer returns something

  1. Open the item (tap My items, then tap the item).
  2. Tap The buyer returned it.
  3. Enter the date and any money you lost (return shipping, fees the site kept).
  4. Tap Record the return. The item goes back into your stock so you can sell it again.

When you donate, toss or keep something

  1. Open the item and tap I got rid of it.
  2. Pick the date and, if you like, say what happened.
  3. Tap Write it off. What you paid counts as a loss, which is normal.

Changed your mind? Open the item and tap Undo — I still have it.

Expenses and mileage

Expenses are things you spend to run the business: shipping supplies, a label printer, app subscriptions, storage, and driving.

  1. Tap Expenses in the menu, then ＋ Add an expense.
  2. Pick what kind it is and enter the amount.
  3. For Mileage / travel, just enter the miles. The dollar amount is worked out for you.
  4. Tap Receipt photo to snap the paper receipt. Very handy at tax time!
  5. Tap Save expense.

Items you buy to resell are not expenses — add those with “I bought something”.

One-time set-up

  • Sites I sell on (on the Home page, at the bottom): eBay, Poshmark, Mercari and others are already there. Tap Edit on each one you use and enter its fees, so estimates are right.
  • Places I buy: add the address of stores you visit often to get a map and directions.

Phone tips

  • Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. iPhone: tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”. Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then “Add to Home screen”.
  • Typing a date? Tap the date box and a calendar pops up.
  • A green message at the top of the page means your changes were saved. A box with “Almost there!” means something needs fixing — look for the red writing.

What the numbers mean

Profit (on an item)
Sale price + shipping the buyer paid, minus what you paid, fees, your shipping label and any other costs.
Profit after expenses
Profit from everything you sold, minus your business expenses. This is what you really kept.
Money tied up in stock
What you paid for everything you haven't sold yet.
Return on what you paid
Profit compared with what you paid. 100% means you doubled your money.
Written off
Items you won't sell. What you paid for them counts as a loss.
